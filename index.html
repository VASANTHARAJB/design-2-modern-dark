About the Journal

Jbel-journal.org is a peer-reviewed academic platform that publishes original research articles, review papers and scholarly resources. We support researchers, faculty and students with a fair, fast and transparent publication process.

Every submission goes through double-blind peer review and plagiarism screening. Accepted papers are published online in open access so knowledge reaches readers everywhere.
